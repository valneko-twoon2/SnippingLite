# Snipping Lite

Snipping Lite is a native Windows screenshot utility. Its icon combines a blue capture frame with red and blue scissors. Start the release build with `pwsh -File scripts/run-latest.ps1` or run `scripts/build.cmd` for a debug build. It starts in the notification area without opening a window; keep it running to use **Alt+Shift+S**. Open settings from the tray menu or the capture toolbar gear. Settings and history both show "Snipping Lite" in the title bar. The settings window opens in its own process (built with egui) and closing it leaves Snipping Lite in the notification area; use the tray menu to reopen settings or exit. The settings window needs about 70 MB while it is open; the tray process itself stays small.

The screen stays live while you snip, so videos and apps keep running and only the selection outline is drawn; turn on "切り取り中に画面を暗くして静止する" (dim and freeze the screen while snipping) in settings to freeze and dim it instead. Select screenshot or video in the toolbar at the top of the screen; the area dropdown selects rectangle, freeform, window, or full-screen capture for either type; the last mode is kept until the app exits. Press Escape to cancel, or use the gear button to open settings. The video button in the toolbar records the selected area to an MP4 file in the Videos folder's "画面録画" (Screen Recordings) folder, the same default as the Windows Snipping Tool (H.264, 60 fps by default with a 30 fps option, video only without audio, optional mouse cursor). Turn off "録画にマウスカーソルを含める" (include the mouse cursor in recordings) to omit the pointer, and choose 30 or 60 fps under "録画のフレームレート" (recording frame rate). Actual throughput depends on the capture size and available processing capacity. Choose Window in the area dropdown and click a window to record it; "ウィンドウを録画するときタイトルバーを含めない" (leave out the title bar when recording a window, on by default) trims its title bar. Freeform videos use the shape's rectangular bounds and fill the area outside the shape with black, since H.264 does not support transparency. After the selection a 3, 2, 1 countdown runs (turn "録画前にカウントダウンする" (countdown before recording) off in settings; click the countdown to cancel). A red frame outlines the area and a small indicator with the elapsed time appears at the top of the screen; neither is part of the video; select it, press the shortcut again, click the tray icon, or choose "録画を停止" (stop recording) in the tray menu to finish, and the notification opens the file. After a capture, a notification appears in the lower-right corner; select "マークアップと共有" (markup and share) to open the newest screenshot in the history editor, or "開く" (open) to open the saved file when history is off. The toolbar also provides editor and history buttons. Settings can automatically copy captures to the clipboard and/or save PNG files to the Pictures folder's "スクリーンショット" (Screenshots) folder, the same default as the Windows Snipping Tool. The save locations can be changed, or opened in Explorer, from the settings window. Enable "保存時にファイル名を指定する" (choose a file name when saving) to pick the name and PNG or BMP format in a Save As dialog. A capture delay of 3, 5, or 10 seconds hides the window before the toolbar appears. At least one destination must be enabled. Every capture is also kept in a history (the newest 50, 100, 200, or 500 as chosen in settings, stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Snipping Lite\history`) that you can browse from the tray menu or the settings window: filter the gallery by date range or search it by date, open a screenshot to copy it again, save it as a file, show it in its folder, or delete it, and clear the whole history at once. In an opened screenshot, the pen and highlighter (five colors, three thicknesses, remembered between sessions) draw on the image and Crop cuts it down; "変更を保存" (save changes) writes the result into the stored image, and Copy and Save as include it. Turn "スクリーンショットの履歴を残す" (keep a history of screenshots) off in settings to stop recording; screenshots can contain sensitive information, so clear the history when you no longer need it. A shortcut conflict is shown in the settings window. The interface is in Japanese only; the settings window follows the Windows Snipping Tool settings layout (sections, On/Off labels). All texts go through `src/i18n.rs`, where another language table can be added.

The toolbar and notification follow the Windows 11 Snipping Tool style, including the light or dark app theme. The settings window imitates the Windows 11 settings style (toggles, cards, the system theme, and the Mica backdrop on Windows 11 22H2 or later) but is not a pixel-exact copy; tooltips and audio recording are not implemented, and annotation is only available for screenshots opened from the history. The outside of a freeform capture is transparent in PNG files and in applications that read transparency from the clipboard, and white elsewhere (including BMP files).
